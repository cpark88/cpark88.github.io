Ph.D. in AI, Lead ML Researcher and Team Lead, SK Telecom

I build AI systems that keep learning from the live services they power.

I am a Lead ML Researcher and Team Lead at SK Telecom, where my team builds and runs the recommender systems and LLMs behind SKT’s apps and services. I joined SKT as a data scientist in 2018 and received my Ph.D. from the Kim Jaechul Graduate School of AI at KAIST in 2024, advised by Jaegul Choo. Before that, I earned a B.A. in Applied Statistics and an M.S. in Industrial Engineering from Yonsei University, where So Young Sohn advised my master’s research.

My research is on self-improving AI systems that learn from the outcomes of their own decisions. I take problems from production, turn them into research questions, and test the answers in large-scale online A/B tests. This work spans cross-domain recommendation, LLM-based recommenders, and reinforcement learning, and now focuses on feedback-driven adaptive AI, real-time and efficient AI, and agentic work automation.
